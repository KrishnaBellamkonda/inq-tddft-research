## Assumptions still in play

- INQ `density::total` correctly k-sums for our 8-kpoint single-rank
  setup (verified via ∫ρ = 162 in Phase 1, ∫ρ = 48 in Phase S2).
- The 2×2×2 shifted MP grid is sufficiently dense for the FFT-peak

  −0.78 eV from QBall could be partly a residual k-grid effect).

  enough that 15 fs of propagation does not introduce drift comparable
  to ΔE_plateau. Spot-check pending.
- Li ONCV PBE-1.2 from SG15 is the same family as the QBall `.xml` (it
  is — same name, same generator), and any small numerical differences
  in the underlying file do not move the FFT peak by > 0.1 eV.
  Untested.
