## 4. WP velocity decreases non-linearly

![WP velocity vs time](attachments/2026-05-05_run_base_n162_L50_E1p5/wp_velocity_vs_time.png)

![WP position vs time](attachments/2026-05-05_run_base_n162_L50_E1p5/wp_position_vs_time.png)

Per the handover, the WP velocity (read from cod_z slope) drops
**0.373 → 0.103 Bohr/a.u.** over 30 a.u., implying a kinetic-energy
loss of **−1.75 eV**. The deceleration is **non-linear** —
slowing-down increases with time until interference effects emerge —
the same shape as the N=138 entry.

> **Open question 1 — is `cod_z` slope really tracking the WP speed?**
> See `docs/plans/jellium_orthonormalisation_rerun.md §4` for a
> per-orbital cod test. If bath orbital cods are stationary, the
> mapping `Δcod_z(total) → v_WP` is straightforward. If they drift,
> the cod-as-WP-velocity interpretation needs revisiting.

> **Open question 2 — physical mechanism for the deceleration.** The
> kinetic-energy *gain* (+0.50 eV) coincides with WP kinetic *loss*
> (−1.75 eV from cod slope), so the **bath kinetic gain is ~+2.25 eV**.
> The "hole-behind-WP attracts WP backwards" Coulombic mechanism is
> **alive and consistent** with this — including with $\Delta E_H < 0$
> — once the projectile sign is taken into account: our WP is a
> *negative* particle, so its density wake is a **depletion**
> (anti-wake), the charge-conjugate of the textbook positive-ion
> accumulation wake. Removing electron density behind a negative
> projectile *lowers* Hartree, so $\Delta E_H < 0$ is exactly what
> this picture predicts. See `plasmons-and-stopping-power.md §6` for
> the full sign-convention argument and the proposed positive-ion
> companion run that will confirm the charge-conjugate symmetry by
> producing the *opposite-sign* density wake at matched velocity.

kinetic up, Hartree down, hole at z ≈ 0.
