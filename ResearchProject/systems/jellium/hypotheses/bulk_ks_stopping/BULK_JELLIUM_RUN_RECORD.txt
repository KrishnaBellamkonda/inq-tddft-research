--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THE CAMPAIGN IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One question: an electron travelling through a homogeneous electron gas is
represented in two ways that are physically matched in every respect except
representation --

  (a) CLASSICAL: a point-like classical particle of mass m_e and charge -1,
      entering the Kohn-Sham Hamiltonian as an EXTERNAL potential (a Gaussian-
      smeared -1 Coulomb pseudopotential), moving by free Ehrenfest dynamics;

  (b) QUANTUM  : a Gaussian WAVEPACKET occupying one additional Kohn-Sham
      orbital of the same TDDFT calculation, propagated by the same
      time-dependent Kohn-Sham equations as the bath.

-- and the electronic stopping power S = -dT/ds is measured for each. The
matched (a)+(b) pair at fixed density and fixed width is called a TWIN PAIR.

The campaign varies TWO axes and holds the projectile velocity FIXED:

  * bath density : 2 values          (r_s = 3.99 and r_s = 5.70)
  * packet width : 3 values          (sigma_WP = 1, 2, 3 Bohr)
  * velocity     : 1 value           (v = 2.71 a.u., i.e. 100 eV) -- NOT swept

  => 2 x 3 = 6 twin pairs = 12 production runs.

THERE IS NO VELOCITY SWEEP IN THIS CAMPAIGN. Every one of the 12 runs is at
100 eV. (Earlier, separate bulk sweeps at other densities did span 20-1100 eV;
they are listed in section 12 and must not be mixed with this set.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PHYSICAL SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Model         : bulk jellium (homogeneous interacting electron gas).
                N valence electrons in a periodic orthorhombic cell, neutralised
                by a UNIFORM positive background.
Boundary      : fully periodic in x, y, z. No surfaces, no absorbing boundary,
                no complex absorbing potential.
Functional    : LDA for the ground state; ALDA (adiabatic LDA) in the real-time
                propagation. Spin-unpolarised.
Background    : uniform => its Hartree potential is pure G = 0, which INQ drops.
                Consequence used throughout the analysis: phi_+ = 0 identically,
                so every background interaction term (E_SB, E_PB, E_BB) is
                exactly zero in bulk (verified bitwise, section 8).
Electron count: N chosen as a closed-shell "magic number" of the free-electron
                gas in the given box, so the ground state is non-degenerate at
                the Fermi level.
Temperature   : 0.00862 eV smearing (100 K) in the ground state.

DERIVED PROPERTIES OF THE TWO BATHS (computed from N and V; the r_s, omega_p
values agree with those stamped in the ground-state summaries):

5. THE PROJECTILE -- THE TWO REPRESENTATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Both carry charge exactly -1 (verified, section 10) and both are launched at
z0 on the cell axis (x = y = 0) moving in +z.

5a. WAVEPACKET HALF ("wp")
    * A normalised Gaussian wavepacket
          psi(r) = (2*pi*sigma_WP^2)^(-3/4) exp(-|r-r0|^2/(4*sigma_WP^2))
                   * exp(i*k0*z)
      is injected into ONE EXTRA Kohn-Sham orbital (state index = n_states-1)
      with occupation 1.0, on top of the doubly-occupied bath orbitals.
    * SIGMA CONVENTION (project-wide, .claude/rules/sigma-wp-convention.md):
      sigma_WP is the width of the WAVEFUNCTION psi. The DENSITY |psi|^2 then
      has standard deviation sigma_WP/sqrt(2). Both are recorded in every
      run_summary.txt as wp_sigma_bohr and wp_sigma_density.
    * k0 = sqrt(2*E/hbar^2) = 2.71106334010243 1/Bohr for E = 100 eV.
    * Orthogonality to the occupied bath was measured, not assumed:
      max overlap with any occupied state is 9.4e-6 / 3.1e-6 / 2.0e-5 / 2.4e-6
      for the four sigma_WP >= 2 runs, and 1.3e-3 / 1.6e-3 for the two
      sigma_WP = 1 runs. Reported per run as 'max_overlap'.
    * Total electrons in the wavepacket run = N + 1 (e.g. 483 for the dense
      bath: 241 bath orbitals x 2 + the packet x 1). Verified from
      occupations_vs_time.csv.

5b. CLASSICAL HALF ("classical")
    * A classical point charge -1 with mass m_e = 1 a.u., entering as an INQ
      "ion" carrying a Gaussian-smeared bare Coulomb pseudopotential:
          UPF file: shared/pseudopotentials/electron_gaussian_wpsigma{1,2,3}p0.upf
      Verified numerically from the UPF mesh for all three files: the local
      potential is exactly
          V_loc(r) = +2 * erf( r / (sqrt(2)*sigma_pot) ) / r   [Rydberg]
      i.e. the potential of a Gaussian charge distribution of charge -1 and
      standard deviation sigma_pot, with sigma_pot = sigma_WP / sqrt(2)
      (max deviation from that closed form: 2.2e-5, 1.1e-5, 7.2e-6 Ry).
      Provenance: derived from anti-proton-ONCV-1.2.upf (itself from
      H_ONCV_PBE-1.2, ONCVPSP; Hamann, PRB 88, 085117 (2013)) by flipping the
      local potential sign, setting z_valence = 0, is_coulomb = T, empty
      nonlocal block, and extending the radial mesh to r_max = 50 Bohr with
      5001 uniform points (dr = 0.01) so the unscreened 1/r tail is represented.
    * WHY sigma_pot = sigma_WP/sqrt(2): it makes the classical charge cloud
      have the SAME standard deviation as the wavepacket's DENSITY. This is
      the matching condition that makes the twin pair a controlled comparison.
      It is validated from the data, not assumed: the projectile self-Hartree
      energy E_PP at t=0 is identical in the two halves to 2.6e-8 eV
      (4.8150 vs 4.8150 eV at sigma_WP=2; 3.0082 vs 3.0082 eV at sigma_WP=3).
      In a paper, report sigma as sigma_WP for BOTH halves; mention sigma_pot
      only in a methods footnote.
    * Ion dynamics: FREE EHRENFEST. The projectile is not driven at constant
      velocity -- it decelerates under the electronic force it induces. This is
      why S is extracted as an initial drag (section 7).

5c. LAUNCH POSITION (depends on sigma so the packet starts clear of the
    periodic face; identical in both halves of a pair):
        sigma_WP = 1  ->  z0 = -36 Bohr
        sigma_WP = 2  ->  z0 = -32 Bohr
        sigma_WP = 3  ->  z0 = -28 Bohr
    (cell is z in [-40, +40].)

7. HOW THE ANALYSIS WINDOW WAS CHOSEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

S is fitted over t in [fit_t0, fit_t1], both stamped into every run_summary.txt
and re-read by the analysis code (never retyped).

  fit_t0 = 4.0 a.u. in every run -- excludes the initial transient during which
           the induced polarisation cloud is still forming.

  fit_t1 = min(t_IFW, t_transverse), where
           t_IFW        = time at which the dispersing packet's leading edge
                          reaches the periodic face in z (dispersion-aware:
                          it accounts for sigma(t) growth, not just the
                          centroid; the static centroid-only rule would give
                          24.34 a.u. in every run and would be too generous).
           t_transverse = time at which the packet's TRANSVERSE spread reaches
                          the x/y faces.

  Resulting windows (a.u.):
      r_s=5.70: sigma1 4.0-10.80 | sigma2 4.0-18.97 | sigma3 4.0-19.48
      r_s=3.99: sigma1 4.0- 9.37 | sigma2 4.0-18.43 | sigma3 4.0-19.48
  A COMMON cross-sigma window of 4.0-9.37 a.u. is also recorded
  (COMMON_FIT_T1 in the JSON files) for like-for-like comparisons across sigma.

  WHY THE FIT IS AN INITIAL DRAG, NOT A STEADY-STATE VALUE (must be stated):
    (i)  the plasma period (28.9 / 49.4 a.u.) is comparable to or longer than
         the run, so no steady-state wake is established; and
    (ii) the projectile is a LIGHT particle under free Ehrenfest dynamics, so it
         decelerates measurably (v/v0 at window end: 0.81-0.93 classical,
         0.96-0.99 wavepacket). A regression over the whole trajectory would
         average S over every velocity between v0 and the final one.
    Both points are project rules: .claude/rules/light-projectile-stopping.md.
    Accordingly the classical half also reports S_cl_initial, restricted to the
    near-constant-velocity window v >= 0.85*v0, with its mean velocity recorded.

  IN-FLIGHT PACKET WIDTH. The wavepacket disperses substantially over the
  window; the width at which it actually does its stopping is NOT sigma_WP.
  Measured density standard deviation sigma_z (Bohr), t=0 -> end of fit window:
      r_s=5.70: sigma1 0.73->8.34 | sigma2 1.41->8.94 | sigma3 2.12->7.94
      r_s=3.99: sigma1 0.77->7.34 | sigma2 1.41->8.68 | sigma3 2.12->8.34
  Any figure plotting S against "sigma" must say whether it means sigma_WP(0)
  or the in-flight width.

9a. THE FOUR WAVEPACKET STOPPING-POWER DEFINITIONS
    Two kinetic-energy definitions x two position definitions:
        T1 = <p^2>/2m       total orbital kinetic energy    (e_kin_ha)
        T2 = <p>^2/2m       drift (centre-of-mass) kinetic energy
        s3 = density centroid of the packet orbital (circular estimator)
        s4 = z0 + integral of <p_z> dt   (Ehrenfest trajectory)
    giving S_ij = -d(T_i)/d(s_j), i in {1,2}, j in {3,4}, by OLS over the fit
    window, in eV/Bohr.

    T1 - T2 = (3/2) * sum_d sigma_pd^2 is the momentum-spread (localisation +
    scattering) energy. It is NOT available to a classical particle, and its
    change along the trajectory is the quantum channel the twin pair exists to
    expose. At t=0 it equals 3/(4*sigma_WP^2) in the psi-width convention these
    runs use (5.10 eV at sigma_WP = 2, confirmed from the data to 6 digits).
    => S_23 (drift KE vs Ehrenfest position) is the like-for-like counterpart
       of the classical number and is the one to quote as "the wavepacket
       stopping power". S_13 additionally charges the packet for its own
       dispersion and is systematically lower.
    s3 and s4 must agree exactly (the KS Hamiltonian here is purely local, so
    d<z>/dt = <p_z>/m holds); their difference is used as a VALIDATION residual,
    not as a physical result.

9b. THE CLASSICAL STOPPING-POWER CHANNELS
    kinetic / S_cl_shared : -d(0.5 m v^2)/dz from electron_track.csv over the
                            same window as the wavepacket half. The primary
                            classical number.
    S_cl_initial          : the same fit restricted to v >= 0.85*v0
                            (initial drag at v0; mean velocity reported).
    methodA               : -d(E_total deposited)/dx with the locked
                            20%-of-time transient cut and free intercept
                            (the project's stopping-power-extraction skill
                            kernel, module stopping_power.py).
    window                : methodA's deposited-energy fit restricted to the
                            wavepacket window, for like-for-like comparison.
    The deposit channel and the kinetic channel agreeing is the energy-
    conservation check; they agree to well under 1% (section 10).

9c. UNCERTAINTIES -- three numbers, three meanings, all in the JSON:
    stat  = OLS standard error of the slope (how well a line is determined).
    syst  = half-spread of the slope as the window edges are moved +/- 3 a.u.
            THIS IS THE DOMINANT AND HONEST UNCERTAINTY -- quote this one.
    r2    = if far below 1, T(s) is not linear over the window and a single S
            does not describe it (report curvature instead).

 1. ONE VELOCITY. Every result here is at v = 2.71 a.u. (100 eV), i.e.
    v/v_F = 5.6 (dense) and 8.1 (dilute). No S(v) curve exists in this dataset.

    comparable to (dense) one plasma period; no steady wake forms.

    by the end of the window). S is quoted at the window-mean velocity, and
    S_cl_initial is provided for the v >= 0.85 v0 restriction.
 4. NOT LINEAR RESPONSE. Local density depletion reaches 68 % (classical) and
    37 % (wavepacket) of n_0. Any comparison to Lindhard theory must say so.
 5. ALDA. The adiabatic local-density kernel has no memory; the wavepacket is
    a single KS orbital and its self-interaction is uncancelled in LDA (this is
    a physical feature of the comparison, quantified as E_PP, not a bug -- but
    it is a named, untested candidate for part of the classical/quantum gap).
 6. sigma_WP = 1 is under-resolved (section 3) and one of its four runs is
    incomplete (section 6).
 7. FINITE BOX. 40-46 Bohr transverse; the packet's transverse width reaches
    6-8 Bohr by the end of the window, and the fit window is truncated at the
    time the packet would touch a face. Self-interaction across periodic images
    is not separately quantified.
